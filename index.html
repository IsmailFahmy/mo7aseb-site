mo7aseb

A personal finance assistant. Mo7aseb (محاسب) means "accountant" in Arabic.

What it does

  • Collects its owner's own bank and card statements: files uploaded by the owner, and statement emails from the owner's banks.
  • Turns each statement into transactions in one ledger, without duplicates.
  • Sorts every transaction into a spending category with rules the owner writes.
  • Shows where the money goes: spend by category and by country, budgets against spend, and recurring subscriptions.

Who it is for

mo7aseb is a private tool. Today it serves one person, its owner, and it is not open for sign-up.

Privacy

mo7aseb reads only its owner's own financial data. It never sells that data, and it shares it only as the privacy policy describes, which also sets out exactly what it accesses and why.
